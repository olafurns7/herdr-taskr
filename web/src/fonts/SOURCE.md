# Fonts

Sofia Sans and Sofia Sans Condensed, copyright 2019 The Sofia Sans Project
Authors (https://github.com/lettersoup/Sofia-Sans), licensed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt; shipped as web/public/fonts/OFL.txt and served at /fonts/OFL.txt).

Built from google/fonts `ofl/sofiasans/SofiaSans[wght].ttf` and
`ofl/sofiasanscondensed/SofiaSansCondensed[wght].ttf` (2026-09-29): weight
axis limited to 350–800 with fontTools.varLib.instancer, then subset with
fontTools.subset to Latin, Latin Extended and punctuation (features kern,
liga, calt, ccmp, locl, mark, mkmk, tnum, pnum, case, frac) as WOFF2.
